# Competitive Analysis & Journey Map (Module 2)

## Responses
- **Role, who are you solving for? (the specific user segment or profile):** The quick closer: a consumer with one or more open claims and variable shift income who opens the portal on her phone after a reminder and wants it done fast.
- **Goal, what is this user ultimately trying to achieve?:** Agree to a monthly plan she can actually keep, so she can stop worrying and get on with her life.
- **Friction, the main barrier (moment of misery) stopping them from succeeding:** She picks the shortest plan because it looks like the right answer, or lets the chat approve one in a minute, without knowing what happens if she misses a payment, and then misses the second one.
- **External tools, the outside platforms or tools the user is forced to use:** The phone line to a collection agent and the paper letters she uses as the reference for her amounts. Assumption, not in the evidence: she checks her banking app to guess what’s left before payday.
- **The process, the 3 to 5 manual steps the user takes to get the job done:** 1 Opens the reminder link on her break and sees only one claim at a time.
2 Guesses an income figure, or switches to the chat for a quick yes.
3 Picks the shortest plan because it looks like the right answer.
4 Misses the second payment, sometimes after a reminder that arrived too late.
5 Calls an agent, who asks again for the details she already entered online.
- **Core frustration, the exact moment the process feels most “broken”:** The moment of confirmation: she says yes without knowing the consequences of a missed payment, and the system treats her case as resolved.
- **The evidence, a specific quote or behavior from the research that proves this:** “I picked the plan with the lowest number of months because it looked like the right answer. The amount was too high and I missed the second payment.” (N-02), backed by the chat confirming plans without missed-payment rules (N-05, T-208) and agents repeating checks (N-08).
- **Your journey map file name (e.g. journey-map.html):** journey-map.md
